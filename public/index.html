Welcome to Just Two

One private chat, made for two browsers. Here’s the quick tour so nothing feels confusing.

🔗

Make it, send it, claim it

1Make a chat and send its invite link to the person you want.
2The invite appears on their home screen. They choose whether to accept it.
3Once accepted, that browser becomes the second spot in the chat.
4Messages, photos and voice notes stay in the chat so you can catch up later.
🎛️

Make it yours

Set a name and photo if you want, pick a theme, change your message colour, react by holding a message, and use the full emoji tray.

Your browser remembers your chat access. Keep unused invite links to people you actually want in the chat.
