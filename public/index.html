How "Just Two" works

1Create a chat and send the invite link to one person.
2The first person who joins becomes the second person in that chat.
3Messages are saved, so either person can send while the other is offline and they can catch up later.
4You can use reactions, photos, voice notes, profile pictures and the typing indicator.
📱

Use it on your devices

Just Two works in a modern browser, so you can use it from a Mac, PC, iPad, tablet or phone. Your chat identity is remembered by that browser.

Tip: keep your invite link private. Anyone who gets an unused invite link could try to claim the second spot.
